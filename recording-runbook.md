# Video Recording Runbook

## Before recording

- Open https://signalledger-ai-builder-cup.web.app in a clean browser window.
- Use a 1440 x 900 or 1600 x 1000 browser window if possible.
- Keep the browser zoom at 100%.
- Do not show Google Cloud credentials, billing screens, terminal windows, or private work material.
- Use English for the submission recording; the Chinese toggle can be shown briefly if useful.

## Exact clicks

1. Let the Discover feed load. Pause on the author cards and public-source labels.
2. Search `HypeMax` in the author search field.
3. Click `Open latest` on the HypeMax card.
4. Show Signal Detail for the original scenario post.
5. Click `Follower Impact` in the left navigation.
6. Click `Author Profile` and show the behavior metrics.
7. Click `Appeal / add context`, then submit the prefilled explanation.
8. Return to Discover and show the public author source link or language toggle.

## Recording rules

- Keep the final video under 3 minutes.
- Use the script in `demo-video-script.md` as the narration.
- Do not call the offline context path “live Gemini” in the narration.
- Say “public delayed market snapshot” for the quote labels.
- Say “scenario author” for HypeMax, ChartSage, and CryptoWhale.
- Say “public commentary” for RSS-sourced authors.
